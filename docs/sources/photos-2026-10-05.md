## What the session covers

Interiors (reception, lounge, chairs, wash stations, products, decor), the six-person team (posed and with mugs), portraits of everyone, cutting and styling at the chair, hot-towel shave with brush and razor, beard oil and face massage, nose and eyebrow waxing, tool details. No exterior or door shot of Oświęcimska 1 and no finished-haircut "results" series; both are still worth shooting.

## Grading pipeline

`magick in.JPG -auto-orient -resize 2600x2600> -colorspace Gray -auto-level -sigmoidal-contrast 3x50% -unsharp 0x1+0.6+0.02 -quality 90 -strip out.jpg` (portraits at 1600, details at 2000). One pipeline for every frame so the set reads as one shoot.
